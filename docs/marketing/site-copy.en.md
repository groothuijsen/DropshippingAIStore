## start-from-zero.md

**title:** Start a Shopify store from zero — Mosaiq
**description:** Describe your niche and get brand-name ideas, a brand kit, product ideas, collections, a menu and standard pages — ready for your own products.

### hero
- headline: From an idea to a store structure in one sitting
- sub: Describe what you want to sell and to whom. Mosaiq proposes a brand, product ideas to import, and builds the store around them. [C-17]

### steps
1. **Describe your niche** — in your own words, plus your markets and price level.
2. **Pick a name** — eight ideas, with a quick domain hint and a link to check trademarks. Or type your own.
3. **Get a brand kit** — tone, style preset, colours and fonts that fit your audience. Adjust anything.
4. **Import products** — Mosaiq suggests what to look for and which EU rules apply. You import with your own app (DSers, CJ, Printify and others) and pick the products. [C-14]
5. **Build** — collections, homepage, product pages, FAQ, shipping and returns pages, and a menu. All as drafts you can review. [C-17]
6. **Go live** — publish what passes the compliance check. Not happy? Undo the build in one click.

### note
Mosaiq doesn't sell or ship products. Your supplier app keeps handling orders — Mosaiq builds the store that sells them.

### faq
- **Do you find winning products?** No — nobody honestly can. Mosaiq suggests product types that fit your niche and flags EU risks; you decide what to import.
- **Will it invent my address or shipping times?** Never. Pages that need company details or delivery times stay in draft until you fill them in.
- **Can I keep my own name?** Yes. Skip the name step and type your own.

## eu-compliance.md

**title:** EU rules for Shopify stores, handled in your pages — Mosaiq
**description:** Omnibus 30-day prices, unit prices, real timers, GPSR, the withdrawal button, AI labels, green claims and delivery times — checked before you publish.

### hero
- headline: The EU rules your product pages must follow — checked before you publish
- sub: Regulators are acting: in a March 2026 EU-wide check of 314 online shops, 30% used misleading discounts. Mosaiq builds the rules into every page. (Source: European Commission, 26 March 2026)

### compliance_grid (rule → what Mosaiq does → claim)
| Rule | What it means | What Mosaiq does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price reductions (Omnibus, Art. 6a PID) | A "was" price must be the lowest price of the 30 days before the discount | Tracks price history and shows only correct prior prices; otherwise no strikethrough [C-07] |
| Unit prices | Price per kg/l/m where required | Shows unit prices, also per bundle tier [C-08] |
| Urgency and scarcity | Timers and "only 3 left" must be true | Timers only for offers with a real end date; stock notices from real stock [C-09] |
| Product safety (GPSR) | Manufacturer, EU responsible person and safety info on the listing | A safety block per product; pages can't go live while it's incomplete [C-10] |
| Withdrawal button (since 19 June 2026) | A clear "withdraw from contract" function | A footer link and a two-step guest form in NL, EN and DE [C-12] |
| AI-generated images (AI Act Art. 50) | AI content must be marked | Visible label plus machine-readable marking [C-11] |
| Green claims (since 27 Sep 2026) | Generic claims like "eco-friendly" are banned | Blocks them before publishing [C-13] |
| Delivery information | Delivery time must be stated before purchase | Delivery times per market on the product page [C-15] |

### note
Mosaiq helps you meet these rules on your product and landing pages. It is not legal advice, and you remain responsible for your store, your terms and your policies.
