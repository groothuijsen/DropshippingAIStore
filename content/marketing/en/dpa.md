## Subject matter and scope

Mosaiq acts as processor for personal data of shoppers that it handles on your behalf: withdrawal requests and GDPR webhook handling.

## Duration, nature and purpose

Processing lasts while the app is installed. Purpose: operating the compliance features you enable.

## Instructions and categories

We process only the categories needed for the features you use, on your instructions (via the app's settings and your store configuration).

## Security

Encryption at rest for stored tokens, EU hosting, access control and regular backups.

## Sub-processors

See the [sub-processors list](/legal/subprocessors/). We notify you of material changes before they take effect.

## Assistance and breaches

We assist with data-subject requests and notify you of personal-data breaches within {hours} hours of becoming aware.

## Deletion and audits

At the end of processing, data is deleted via Shopify's data-request mechanism. You may request an audit summary of our safeguards.
