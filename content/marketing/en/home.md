## Built for EU rules, not bolted on

Since 2024 Europe has tightened the rules for online shops: discounts must be measured against the lowest price of the last 30 days, product safety information is mandatory, a withdrawal button became law in June 2026, and AI images must be marked. Mosaiq checks these on every page before you publish — so your store doesn't copy the mistakes regulators are fining others for.

See the full list on the [EU compliance page](/eu-compliance/).

*Mosaiq helps you meet these rules; you stay responsible for your store.*
