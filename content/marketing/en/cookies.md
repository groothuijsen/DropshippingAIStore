- **CSRF cookie**: set only on pages with a form, strictly necessary for form security. Session-scoped.
- **Analytics**: first-party, cookieless page-view beacon — no cookie, no cross-site identifier.
- **No third-party cookies**: the marketing site loads no external scripts, fonts or trackers.

Because there are no non-essential cookies, no consent banner is shown.
