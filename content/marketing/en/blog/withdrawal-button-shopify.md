## What changed

Directive 2023/2673 requires a clear "withdraw from contract" function for online sales — in effect since 19 June 2026 in most member states (the Netherlands has a 12-month transition for existing stores).

## What it must look like

A visible link or button with an unambiguous label in the language of the customer, a two-step form without an account requirement, and a confirmation of the withdrawal.

## What Shopify covers natively

Shopify handles standard cancellations of unfulfilled orders; it does not build the statutory withdrawal function with confirmation for consumers.

## In practice

Footer link + two-step guest form in NL, EN and DE, with a confirmation message — that is what regulators expect to see.
