## What the directive bans

Generic environmental claims like "eco-friendly", "green" or "sustainable" without proven specifics are prohibited for products sold to consumers.

## What is still allowed

Specific, verifiable claims with recognised excellent performance, and labels backed by a certification scheme the consumer can check.

## Why it matters for copy

Many Shopify stores still carry old supplier copy with banned phrases — publishing them is the store owner's violation.

## How Mosaiq helps

The claim check blocks generic green claims before a page can be published, and suggests specific wording instead.
