## What the rule says

Since the Omnibus Directive (Art. 6a PID), a "was" price must be the lowest price of the 30 days before the discount. Showing a higher reference price is a misleading practice.

## What counts as the prior price

The lowest selling price in the preceding 30 days — not the original launch price, not an inflated list price. Short flash sales do not reset the window.

## CJEU C-330/23 (Aldi Sud)

The Court of Justice confirmed that the reference price must genuinely have been available to consumers in the previous 30 days.

## Enforcement

The ACM fined several webshops in June 2024 for fake reference prices. Regulators across the EU ran sweeps in 2025–2026.

## Bundles vs reductions

A genuine bundle (buy 2, save 10% vs today's price) is not a price reduction — but the saving shown must still be truthful.
