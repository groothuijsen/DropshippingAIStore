## Why dropshippers are in scope

The GPSR (Regulation 2023/988) applies to whoever places a product on the EU market — including dropshippers selling directly to consumers.

## What must be on the listing

Manufacturer name and contact, an EU responsible person where applicable, product identification, and safety information such as warnings.

## The EU responsible person

For many product categories a person or company in the EU must be designated and named on the listing.

## Enforcement

National market surveillance authorities conduct online sweeps; incomplete listings get warnings first, then fines.
