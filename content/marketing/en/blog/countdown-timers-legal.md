## The problem

Fake urgency — countdown timers that reset, stock notices that never change — is a dark pattern under the UCPD and a priority for enforcers.

## What the March 2026 EU sweep found

A large share of checked shops used urgency claims that could not be substantiated with real data.

## When a timer is legal

Only when the offer genuinely ends at the shown time — a real promotional window with a real end.

## When a stock notice is legal

Only when it reflects real stock levels, updated from the system that manages inventory.

## How Mosaiq helps

Timers are only generated for offers with a real end date; stock notices come from real inventory data.
