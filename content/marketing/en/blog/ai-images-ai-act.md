## What Article 50 requires

AI-generated or manipulated content must be marked as such in a machine-readable way, and consumers must be informed where it is not obvious.

## Timeline

Obligations apply from 2 August 2026, with a grace period for certain content until 2 December 2026 — plan for it now.

## C2PA content credentials

C2PA is the emerging standard for machine-readable provenance metadata on images.

## What shoppers must see

A visible label on AI-generated product images, plus machine-readable marking in the file.
