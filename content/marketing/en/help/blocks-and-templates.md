Mosaiq works through Shopify app blocks — your theme files are never edited.

- **mq-price**: price, delivery estimate and cut-off notice. Place it on the product template near the price.
- **mq-page-sections**: renders pages Mosaiq builds (landing pages, PDPs).
- **mq-gpsr**, **mq-bundle-picker**, **mq-cart-drawer**, **mq-tokens**: placed automatically or via the editor.

To remove Mosaiq completely, uninstall the app — Shopify removes the blocks.
