title: "How to install Mosaiq"
slug: "/help/installation/"
description: "Install from the Shopify App Store, approve permissions, connect your supplier app."
template: "marketing/article.html"
---
1. Install Mosaiq from the Shopify App Store and approve the requested permissions.
2. Open the app from your Shopify admin and sign in with your store.
3. Connect your supplier app (DSers, CJ, Zendrop, AutoDS or Printify) so Mosaiq can read products.
4. Run the first build or open the editor for an existing product.

Billing starts after your 7-day trial through your normal Shopify invoice.
