- Billing runs through Shopify on your normal Shopify invoice.
- Every plan includes a 7-day free trial.
- Annual billing costs 10 months.
- Plans have fixed monthly limits; reaching a limit never triggers extra charges — upgrade or wait for the next period.
- Cancel any time by uninstalling the app; billing stops with the current term.

Prices: see the [pricing page](/pricing/).
