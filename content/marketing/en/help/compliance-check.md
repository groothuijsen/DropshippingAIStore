Every page is scored before it can go live. The check covers:

- Omnibus 30-day reference prices
- Unit prices per bundle tier
- Timers and stock notices (only when real)
- GPSR safety block completeness
- Withdrawal function presence
- AI image labelling
- Green-claim screening
- Delivery information per market

Red findings block publication. Fix them in the editor, or ask for the change in plain language.
