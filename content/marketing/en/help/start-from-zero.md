1. Describe your niche, markets and price level.
2. Pick a name from the generated ideas — or type your own.
3. Review the brand kit: tone, style preset, colours, fonts.
4. Import products with your own supplier app.
5. Mosaiq builds collections, pages and menus as drafts for your review.
6. Publish what passes the compliance check.

Mosaiq never invents company details or delivery times — those pages stay drafts until you fill them in.
